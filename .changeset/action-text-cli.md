`patter loc-export` says how many text lines it left out as action and that `localiseDialogueText` is off, then lists the text lines that have dropped out of the loc files (now action) or come into them, by id and scene; `patter loc-import` says how many translations of action text it dropped; `patter report` prints an action lines count; `patter validate` warns when a scene's kind no longer matches its loc files.
