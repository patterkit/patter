The playable HTML page has a Continue toggle, as in Patterpad's Play window and under its stored key (`patter.playContinue`). Off, each beat waits for the reader: a click or tap on the page, Space or Enter, or a Next button; the first line, the reply to a choice, the choices, and the end come at once. On, the page plays through on its timing, as before. With no stored choice, the bundle's `voiced` picks the default: off for an unvoiced story, on for a voiced one. The player adds the toggle to a page published before it existed.
