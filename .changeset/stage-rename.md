`renameStage` carries a quality's stage rename into every condition and set effect that names the stage, across the scenes it can reach, and `stageGateCount` says how many expressions order the quality by its stages, which is what a reorder changes. `renameStageInExpression` and `stageDialect` are the pieces, for a caller with its own expressions.
