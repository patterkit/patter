Defaults are optional in source and resolved in the bundle (the family's rule, ruled 2026-10-18): every property, scene property and game-data field in the bundle carries its default, the one it was given or else its type's by `defaultFor`. The content hashes stay over the source as written, and a host scope's declarations ship as written. A set effect assigning a quality is checked with `validateAssignment`: a stage it names must be one of that quality's.
