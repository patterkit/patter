Action text: the project setting `localiseDialogueText` (off by default), `action: true` on a bundle's text beat, `textAction` on a translated loc shard, and the helpers `isDialogueScene` and `textIsAction`. A dialogue scene is one with a line naming a character, an option's prompt included. With the setting off, its text beats are action: a direction for whoever stages the scene, never shown to the player, and never localised. A text scene's text, and an option's prompt, are localised whatever the setting says.
