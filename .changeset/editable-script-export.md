`exportEditableScript` writes the editable script: the readable script with every editable line's words in a shaded box beside a `[#K7Q2M]` marker, a front page explaining how to edit it and read its structure, and the handoff id in the page header, plus the handoff record to commit. It opens and edits in Word, Google Docs, OnlyOffice, and Pages.
